01 — What you are looking at

Gear ratios, not spinning circles.

Five concentric rings turn at fixed integer ratios — −1 : +2 : −3 : +6 : −12 — which is what makes clockwork read as a mechanism rather than as decoration. Because the ratios are whole numbers the rings drift apart and periodically realign, exactly as a real gear train does. The escapement ring doesn't glide: it advances in 60 discrete steps, and that single stepped element is what sells the whole thing.

It is drawn procedurally to Canvas at your device's full pixel ratio, so it is razor-sharp on any screen and weighs nothing — no video file, no image sequence, no 3D library. Scroll the page and the mechanism advances with you.

02 — The hard case

Now the glass is over paper.

This is the section that breaks most glass navs. Bone ink on a light ground has nothing to hold onto, so the pill has to carry its own contrast rather than borrow it from the page. Scroll slowly through this boundary and check the nav stays readable the whole way.

Two colours in this brand are traps, and they get enforced in the schema rather than noticed in review. Washed black #171717 on the ground #0F0F0F is 1.07:1 — invisible. Washed grey #A7A5A4 on this paper #DFDDDE is 1.84:1 — also invisible. So every colourway carries a required ground assignment that decides which background it may be photographed against and displayed on. Fixed at generation time, not patched later in CSS.

  • #171717 washed black → paper only
  • #A7A5A4 washed grey → dark only

03 — Where the mechanism came from

It was already on the shirt.

The orbital globe above — concentric rings, orbit ellipses, a compass star, Roman numerals on the clock pocket print — is your cousin's own artwork. The mechanism doesn't import a look from somewhere else; it sets that drawing turning. The Capaldi titles gave the structure: concentricity, meshing rates, the lock-into-place. What they didn't give is the colour — those titles are gold, amber and high-contrast sci-fi, and this brand is bone on near-black, washed and quiet. We took the mechanism and left the palette.

The word space is the fragile part. Regenerating the garment renders proved the name collapses to OURTIME under the slightest pressure — it happened on the jeans twice before it held. On the site the wordmark is artwork, never a webfont. Where it is set in type, a CI assertion measures the rendered gap between the R and the T and fails the build below 0.5em. The panel is measuring that live.
