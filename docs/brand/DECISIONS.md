## 1. The Black Isle positioning is WITHDRAWN — 16 Sep 2026

**Decision (Jono):** the brand is **not** to be positioned as a Highland/Scottish
label. That is **Oliver's call to make**, not ours, and it has not been made.

**Therefore, do not use, and strip on sight:**
- "From the Black Isle" as a brand line, origin story or positioning.
- The Highland-light narrative (18-hour midsummer days vs 6.5-hour midwinter).
- Location photography direction built on sea, firths, haar or Highland weather.
- Any colour rationale that names a real place (the ledger has been rewritten).
- The coordinates as a *brand signature* — in the footer, on the site, in copy.

**What survives, and why it is not a contradiction:**
- **The 24-hour colour index stays.** It is a TIME system. It never needed the
  place — a colour is assigned the hour it looks like, full stop.
- **The coordinates stay on the garment.** Jono specifically asked for the globe
  tee's print to be changed to `57.5878° N, 4.2383° W`, and that is a product
  decision already taken and already regenerated. The site describes that tee
  factually and builds nothing on it.
- **The whole time theme stays.** `OUR TIME.` `EARN EVERY SECOND.`
  `TIME WON'T WAIT.` `DISCIPLINE. FOCUS. CONSISTENCY.` `24 / 7` `EST. 2026`.

**Affected sections, which are now partly void:**
`strategy.md` §3 and everything downstream of it · `visual.md` §5 location
photography · `applications.md` §5–6 wherever place is the content hook ·
`motion.md` where the light narrative is used as justification.
The rest of those sections stands.

**If Oliver later decides he does want it:** nothing has been destroyed. The
argument is intact in `strategy.md` §3 and can be reinstated as a whole.

## 2. Care instructions — ONE canonical line

The draft specified four different care blocks, two of them mutually exclusive
(`TUMBLE DRY LOW` against `DO NOT TUMBLE DRY`). On garment-dyed heavyweight that
is a live consumer-law exposure, not a style inconsistency.

**Canonical, and the only version that may be printed or published:**

> WASH COLD 30°. INSIDE OUT. HANG DRY. DO NOT TUMBLE DRY. COOL IRON — NOT ON PRINT.

With the one voice line beneath it: *It will fade. That is the point.*

## 3. Launch range — Commercial overrides Strategy

`strategy.md` proposes ~14 pieces; `commercial.md` costs a launch of **3 garment
types, 6 colourways, 320 units, ≈£10,800**. Commercial wins — it is the only one
with the maths behind it. Note the two sections also use "SKU" to mean different
things (colourway vs colourway×size).

**Precedence, in general:** Commercial decides what gets made and when. Visual
decides how anything looks. Verbal decides what anything is called.

## 4. Known error — the price architecture ignores VAT

`commercial.md` §1.3–1.5 treats RRP as revenue throughout, so every margin and
multiple in it is overstated in the same direction. **Do not act on those figures
until they are reworked ex-VAT.**
